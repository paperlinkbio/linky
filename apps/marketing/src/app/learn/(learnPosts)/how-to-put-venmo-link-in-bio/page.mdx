To put a Venmo link in your bio, copy your Venmo profile link, which looks like `https://venmo.com/u/yourname`, and paste it into your bio or add it as a link on your link-in-bio page. People who tap it land on your Venmo profile, ready to pay you.

## Find your Venmo link

1. Open the Venmo app and tap the **Me** tab.
2. Tap your **profile** and choose to share it, then copy the link.

You can also type it yourself: it's `https://venmo.com/u/` followed by your Venmo username.

## Add it to your Linky page

1. Open your page in the Linky editor.
2. Add a **Link Box** or **Link Bar** block.
3. Paste your Venmo link into the **Link** field and give it a clear label, such as "Tip me on Venmo" or "Pay for your booking".
4. Publish your page.

Putting Venmo on a [link-in-bio page](/i/learn/what-is-a-link-in-bio) rather than directly in your profile means you keep your one bio link free for everything else, and payments sit next to your booking, shop and social links.
