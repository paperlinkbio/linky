A link in bio is the single website link in a social media profile, the one a creator means when they say "link in bio" in a post. Because Instagram, TikTok and most other apps only give you one or a few profile links, most people point that link at a link-in-bio page: a small, mobile-friendly page that lists all their links and content in one place.

## How a link-in-bio page works

1. You create a page with a tool such as [Linky](/i/learn/what-is-linky) and get a short URL, like `lin.ky/yourname`.
2. You paste that URL into the website field of each social profile.
3. When followers tap it, they see all your links: your shop, latest video, newsletter, other socials and anything else you add.
4. When something changes, you update the page once instead of editing every profile.

## What people put on one

Most pages lead with whatever matters right now, such as a new release or an event, and keep the evergreen links below that. See [what should I put in my link in bio?](/i/learn/what-should-i-put-in-my-link-in-bio) for ideas.

## Do you need one?

If you only ever share one destination, the profile link on its own is fine. As soon as you want to send people to more than one place, a link-in-bio page saves you from swapping the link every time you post. You can [make one for free](/i/learn/how-to-make-a-link-in-bio-for-free) in a few minutes.
