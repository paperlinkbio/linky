To change the link in your Instagram bio, go to your profile, tap **Edit profile**, then **Links**. Tap the link you want to change, edit the URL or title, and tap **Done** or the checkmark to save. The new link shows on your profile immediately.

## Step by step

1. Open Instagram and tap your **profile picture** in the bottom-right corner.
2. Tap **Edit profile**, then **Links**.
3. Tap the link you want to update.
4. Edit the URL or title. To remove it instead, tap **Remove link**.
5. Tap **Done** (or the checkmark) to save.

## Stop changing it every time you post

If you change your bio link whenever you have something new to share, point it at a [link-in-bio page](/i/learn/what-is-a-link-in-bio) instead. Your Instagram link then stays the same, and you update the page behind it: put the new release or product at the top and older links further down.

With Linky, [live blocks](/i/integrations) can do some of that for you. They show your latest posts or what you're listening to without any editing.
