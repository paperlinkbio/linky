To add a link to your TikTok bio, go to your profile, tap **Edit profile**, and paste your URL into the **Website** field, then save. The link appears under your bio.

The catch is that TikTok doesn't show the Website field to everyone. You'll see it if you have a business account, or a personal account with at least 1,000 followers. If you don't see it, you can switch to a business account in your settings.

## Step by step

1. Open TikTok and go to your **Profile**.
2. Tap **Edit profile**.
3. Tap **Website** and paste your URL, for example `lin.ky/yourname`.
4. Tap **Save**.

## If there's no Website field

1. On your profile, tap the **menu** (three lines) in the top-right corner, then **Settings and privacy**.
2. Tap **Account**, then **Switch to Business Account**, and follow the steps.
3. Go back to **Edit profile**. The **Website** field should now be there.

## Make the one link count

TikTok gives you a single bio link, so most creators use a [link-in-bio page](/i/learn/what-is-a-link-in-bio) that holds everything else. Linky can also show your [latest TikTok videos](/i/integrations/tiktok) on that page automatically, and the [TikTok generator](/i/tiktok) can build a page from your profile for you.
