Yes. Linky's free plan gives you one page with up to five blocks, including the live [Instagram](/i/integrations/instagram) and [Spotify](/i/integrations/spotify) blocks and custom themes. Your page stays live on the free plan for as long as you like, and you don't need a credit card to sign up.

Every new account also starts with 14 days of Premium, so you can try the paid features before deciding.

## What each plan includes

| Plan    | Price     | What you get                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free    | $0        | One page, up to 5 blocks, Instagram and Spotify integrations, custom themes                                                                                                                                                        |
| Premium | $4/month  | Unlimited pages and blocks, [custom domains](/i/learn/can-i-use-my-own-domain), [analytics](/i/learn/how-to-track-link-in-bio-clicks), private pages, a [verified badge](/i/learn/how-to-get-a-verified-badge-on-your-link-in-bio) |
| Team    | $14/month | Everything in Premium, a separate team space, and up to 5 team members                                                                                                                                                             |

## Do I need to upgrade?

Only if you need more than five blocks, want to use your own domain, or want to see how many people visit your page and where they come from. If your page is a handful of links and a Spotify or Instagram block, the free plan covers it.

See the full breakdown on the [pricing page](/i/pricing).
