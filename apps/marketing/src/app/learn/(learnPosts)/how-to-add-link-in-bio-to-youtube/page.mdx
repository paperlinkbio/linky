To add links to your YouTube channel, open **YouTube Studio**, go to **Customization**, and add them in the **Links** section. Give each link a title and URL, then click **Publish**. Your links appear on your channel page, under your channel description.

## Step by step

1. Go to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com) and sign in.
2. Click **Customization** in the left menu.
3. Find the **Links** section and click **Add link**.
4. Enter a title and the URL, for example `lin.ky/yourname`.
5. Click **Publish**.

You can also put links in individual video descriptions, which is often where viewers look for them.

## Keep your links in one place

Updating every video description whenever something changes isn't practical. Put a single [link-in-bio page](/i/learn/what-is-a-link-in-bio) in your channel links and your video descriptions, then keep that page up to date. Your merch, socials, newsletter and latest release stay one tap away, and the link in old videos never goes stale.
