Instead of "link in bio", say what people will get when they tap: "the full video is at the link in my profile", "tickets are in my bio", "grab the free guide from my profile". Naming the reward gives followers a reason to go and look, which a generic "link in bio" doesn't.

## Alternatives to try

- "New single out now, listen via my profile."
- "Book your slot at the link on my page."
- "Only 20 left, shop through my bio before they go."
- "I wrote up everything I learned. It's linked on my profile."
- "Tap my profile for the recipe."

The pattern is the same each time: name the thing, then say where it is.

## Make the link live up to the promise

Whatever you say, the page people land on should show that thing first. If your post promises tickets, put the ticket link at the top of your [link-in-bio page](/i/learn/what-is-a-link-in-bio), not halfway down. See [how to get more clicks on your link in bio](/i/learn/how-to-get-more-clicks-on-your-link-in-bio) for more on that.
