To add a link to your Instagram bio, go to your profile, tap **Edit profile**, then **Links** and **Add external link**. Paste the URL, give it a title, and tap **Done** or the checkmark to save. The link appears under your bio straight away.

Instagram lets you add up to five links this way. If you want to share more than that, or change your links often, add a single [link-in-bio page](/i/learn/what-is-a-link-in-bio) instead so you only ever update one place.

## Step by step

1. Open Instagram and tap your **profile picture** in the bottom-right corner.
2. Tap **Edit profile**.
3. Tap **Links**, then **Add external link**.
4. Paste your URL, for example `lin.ky/yourname`, and add a title.
5. Tap **Done** (or the checkmark) to save.

## Tips

- Put the link to your link-in-bio page first, so it's the one people see before tapping "and 2 more".
- Mention the link in your captions, and use a [link sticker in your stories](/i/learn/how-to-add-a-link-to-your-instagram-story).
- Check it works by tapping it from another account. If it doesn't open, see [why is my link in bio not working?](/i/learn/why-is-my-link-in-bio-not-working)

Need more than five links? See [how to add multiple links to your Instagram bio](/i/learn/how-to-add-multiple-links-to-instagram-bio).
