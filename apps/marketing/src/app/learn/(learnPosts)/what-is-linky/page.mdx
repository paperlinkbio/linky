Linky is an open-source link-in-bio builder. You get one link, like `lin.ky/yourname`, to put in your Instagram, TikTok, X or YouTube bio. It opens a page that holds everything you want to share: your links, your shop, your newsletter and live content from your other accounts. The code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/trylinky/linky).

## What makes Linky different

Most link-in-bio tools give you a list of buttons. Linky pages are built from blocks, and some of those blocks update themselves:

- **Live integrations.** Show what you're playing on [Spotify](/i/integrations/spotify), your latest [Instagram](/i/integrations/instagram) posts or [TikTok](/i/integrations/tiktok) videos, and your [GitHub](/i/integrations/github) activity.
- **Themes you control.** Start from a [template](/i/templates) and change the colours, fonts and background to match your brand.
- **Your own domain.** On Premium, your page can load on an address you own. See [can I use my own domain?](/i/learn/can-i-use-my-own-domain)
- **Analytics.** Premium shows how many people visit your page, where they come from and which devices they use.

## How much does it cost?

Linky is free to start: one page with up to five blocks. Premium is $4/month and Team is $14/month. See [is Linky free?](/i/learn/is-linky-free) for what each plan includes.

## Who uses it?

Creators, [musicians](/i/for/musicians), [photographers](/i/for/photographers), small businesses and anyone who needs more than the single link a social profile allows. Browse real pages on [Explore](/i/explore).
